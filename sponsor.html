Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about child sponsorship

How long does sponsorship last?

Sponsorship typically continues until the child completes their education or until you choose to end your support. The average sponsorship lasts 5-7 years.

Can I communicate with the child I sponsor?

Yes! You can exchange letters and photos through our secure portal. We also offer translation services if needed.

What percentage of my donation goes to the child?

85% of your donation goes directly to program services for the child and their community. 10% supports administrative costs, and 5% goes toward fundraising efforts.

Can I visit the child I sponsor?

Yes, we organize sponsor visits to our program areas. These visits are arranged in advance and follow our child protection policies.
