Our Beginnings

Homez Charity Org was founded in 2005 with a simple yet powerful vision: to ensure that every child, regardless of their circumstances, has access to quality education and a chance for a better future.

What began as a small initiative to support 15 children in a single community has grown into a movement that has transformed thousands of lives across multiple regions.

Our Growth

Over the years, we've expanded our programs to include not just educational sponsorship but also orphanage support, community outreach, and empowerment initiatives.

Today, we continue to be driven by the belief that education is the most powerful tool for breaking the cycle of poverty and creating lasting change in communities.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide everything we do

Compassion

We approach our work with empathy and kindness, recognizing the inherent dignity of every individual we serve.

Integrity

We maintain the highest ethical standards, ensuring transparency and accountability in all our operations.

Empowerment

We focus on creating opportunities that enable people to transform their own lives and communities.

Collaboration

We believe in the power of partnership and work closely with communities, donors, and other organizations.

Our Mission & Vision

Guiding our journey to create lasting change

Our Mission

To provide underprivileged children with access to quality education, resources, and opportunities that empower them to break the cycle of poverty and build a brighter future.

Our Vision

A world where every child has equal access to education and the opportunity to realize their full potential, regardless of their socioeconomic background.
